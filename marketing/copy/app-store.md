## Long description (~600 chars)

GOLAZO is live in-play betting built around the next moment of a soccer match.

Forget the final score. Bet on what happens next: the next shot, the next corner, the next goal, the next booking. A market drops, you pick a side, and it settles in seconds. Then the next one opens.

It's parimutuel, so there's no house line to beat. Everyone backing a moment goes into one pool, the odds shift live as money moves, and the winners split the pot.

Watch the pool fill, see sides swing, and climb the board against everyone reading the same match.

The next moment is always coming. Be on it. GOLAZO is live.
