# GOLAZO — Press

## One-line press blurb

GOLAZO is a live in-play betting app that lets fans bet on the next moment of a soccer match, the next shot, corner, goal or booking, in parimutuel pools that settle in seconds.

## About (one paragraph)

GOLAZO is live in-play betting built around a single idea: the next moment. Instead of wagering on the final score and waiting 90 minutes, fans bet on what happens next in a live match, the next shot, corner, goal or booking, in fast parimutuel pools that settle in seconds. There's no house line to beat. Everyone backing a moment goes into one pool, the odds move live as money flows in, and the winners split the pot. With a live board and pools you can watch fill in real time, GOLAZO turns every minute of a match into a market and the people watching into players.
