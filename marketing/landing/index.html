FAQ

Quick answers.

QWhat does parimutuel actually mean?

Everyone who backs a moment pays into one shared pot. When the moment resolves, the pot is split between the people who got it right. There is no fixed bookmaker price, the odds you see come from how the crowd is betting.

QHow fast do bets settle?

In seconds. A market is tied to a single live moment, so the instant it happens on the pitch the pool resolves and winnings land. No waiting for full time.

QDo I need to download an app?

No. GOLAZO runs in your browser. Open golazo.wooblay.com, find a live match and you are in.
